What to check before ordering

Match the size and variant

Open the item on Hipobuyfind and check the seller’s size chart. For clothes, measure a garment you already wear flat across the chest and compare its length. For shoes, check what the seller’s chart measures before comparing centimeters.

Review warehouse QC photos

If your agent provides quality-control photos, compare the size label, color and visible condition with the option you ordered. Check the return deadline and ask about discrepancies before approving shipment.

Estimate the total delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international freight. Check whether the shipping line uses actual or volumetric weight, and account for any applicable taxes and duties at your destination.

Sugargoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is a catalog of product links used to find and compare items before ordering through a shopping agent. This page is an independent guide that uses Hipobuyfind listings as its source.

How do I open a listing with Sugargoo?

Open an item in the catalog, select Buy Link on Hipobuyfind and choose Sugargoo if it is listed. Confirm the product, variant and current price with the agent before paying.

Does the catalog price include international shipping?

The catalog shows reference item prices. The agent quotes shipping separately based on your destination, parcel measurements and available shipping lines.

Are the product images warehouse QC photos?

These are catalog images. If your agent provides warehouse QC photos, review the images for your specific order before approving shipment.
